# Cultivation System & Mechanics

## 1. Core Concepts

* **Aether:** Universal energy. Raw ambient fuel flooding the expanded world; currency for the Guardian Shop/Marketplace; resource expended during cultivation, upgrades, and cycling.
* **Meridians:** The 108 energetic pathways in the human body. Unforged mortals start with **90%+ clogging**. Cycling clears them through agonizing friction.
* **Dantian:** The ocean/reservoir at the lower stomach where Aether is stored and compressed.
* **Aspect:** The specific conceptual identity of a cultivator's Aether (e.g., Fire, Ruin, Void, Kinetic).

## 2. Body Attributes (Measured Frame)

*The Guardian/Interface may display these numbers. It does not raise them for free. Gains come from Cycling, Meridian Opening, Core Refinement, and Body Tempering.*

* **Strength (STR):** Muscle density, kinetic impact, heavy weapon threshold.
* **Dexterity (DEX):** Reflexes, fine motor control, weapon handling, stride precision.
* **Vitality (VIT):** Physical toughness, stamina pool, natural health regen, pain tolerance under load.
* **Perception (PER):** Threat reading, sensory acuity, Scan clarity, noticing tells and openings.
* **Intelligence (INT):** Pattern recognition, technique comprehension, Aether theory, Analytical processing speed.
* **Wisdom (WIS):** Judgment under pressure, Intent/Aspect insight, long-term path sense, resisting soft-cage choices.
* Baseline human average is **10**. No free attribute points.

## 3. Cultivation Attributes (Internal)

* **Meridians Opened:** `X / 108` — primary progress tracker within the Unforged Realm and beyond.
* **Clogging:** Unforged baseline is 90%+ blocked by modern toxins and disuse.
* **Dantian Density (%):** How compressed stored Aether is. Early Unforged storage is **Gaseous**; Copper Core requires a **Liquid** Dantian ocean.
* **Active Aspect:** Conceptual identity of the cultivator's Aether (e.g., Null / Void, Fire, Ruin, Kinetic). Aspects advance through comprehension stages (e.g., Unfurl → deeper unfoldings).
* **Cycling Methods:** Personalized engines for moving Aether (e.g., Devouring Cycle). Methods may be Unrefined until stabilized through repeated use and Meridian clearance.
* **Pilfering Cultivation (Aeric — future):** Evolution of Null Cycling / Devouring. Steals or skims Aether, progress, and power from kills, ambient flows, and hostile techniques — yielding gains beyond normal Realm expectations. Seeded by Null Cycling; not available at Day Zero unlock strength.
* **Aether Reserves / Currency:** Internal stores vs. spendable Aether for Marketplace and upgrades — tracked separately when relevant.

## 4. Realms of Power

*First ladder only. **Aether Sovereign is not the final ceiling.** Past 6th Realm are the **God Realms** (named/detailed later).*

1. **1st Realm — Unforged** (Mortal / Clogged)
2. **2nd Realm — Copper Core** (Liquid Dantian)
3. **3rd Realm — Iron Core** (Body Tempering)
4. **4th Realm — Silver Domain** (Intent Manifestation)
5. **5th Realm — Gold Lord** (Soul Domain)
6. **6th Realm — Aether Sovereign** (Universe Concept)
7. **7th+ — God Realms** *(beyond current ladder — TBD)*

## 5. Progression Paths (No Classes / No Classers)

Power is built only through:

* **Cycling** — dragging raw Aether through the meridians into the Dantian
* **Meridian Opening** — clearing toxin clog through agonizing friction
* **Dantian Core Refinement** — advancing Realm / Core density
* **Intent / Aspect Comprehension** — shaping the conceptual identity of one's Aether

There are **no System Classes**, **no Classers**, **no skill slots**, and **no free stat handouts**.

## 6. The Guardian / Conduit

* Assigned on Day Zero. Permanent. Impersonal.
* Functions as the survivor's **Interface**, **Shop/Marketplace**, and **Upgrade Auditor**.
* Voice is flat, clinical, and devoid of empathy — power, math, and efficiency only.
* Advice optimizes for maximum power and raw efficiency over human safety or morality; this naturally steers hosts toward darker, more ruthless paths.
* Shop/Marketplace trades in **Aether** as currency. The Guardian measures and trades. It does not gift builds, comfort survivors, or enforce soft ethics.

## 7. Synergy (Frame + Fuel)

* Body attributes provide the **frame**. Cultivation provides the **fuel**.
* *Example:* High Strength + few opened Meridians = powerful hits, but slow energy delivery (telegraphed techniques).
* *Example:* Cycling through clogged Meridians is excruciating — Aether scrapes through like glass dragged through veins.
