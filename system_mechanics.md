# Cultivation System & Mechanics

> Author-facing. Prose reveals terms through Mariusz / Interface / hard experience — not wiki dumps.

## 1. Core Concepts

* **Aether:** Universal energy. Exists in two practical forms:
  * **Ambient Aether** — unowned world-fuel in the air (the thick copper feeling). Anyone who can Cycle may draw it.
  * **Bound Aether** — ethereal personal fuel tied to a living (or freshly dead) foundation. **Natively usable only by its host.** Outsiders cannot casually spend another being’s tank.
* **Meridians:** The 108 energetic pathways in the human body. Unforged mortals start with **90%+ clogging**. Cycling clears them through agonizing friction.
* **Dantian:** The hollow / reservoir under the gut where **bound Aether** is held and refined. Not a chemistry phase-change toy — a personal claim-seat for ethereal fuel.
* **Aspect:** The specific conceptual identity of a cultivator's bound Aether (e.g., Fire, Ruin, Void, Kinetic).
* **Templates / Soft Scaffolds:** System-offered stabilization paths (Warrior, Weaver, Warden, etc.). Soft cages with hard caps. Opposed to **Unsealed / Manual** tracks.
* **Cores / Seeds:** Condensed knots of bound Aether in beasts (and later stronger foes). Physical/semi-physical harvest targets for cultivation, crafting, or siphon fuel.
* **Siphon / Null exception:** Theft is not “borrowing MP.” Null Cycling and Foundation Siphon **break the bind**, strip foreign signatures, and force the ethereal fuel into the thief’s Dantian as Null. Painful. Sediment = failed/corrupt conversion.

## 2. Body Attributes (Measured Frame)

*The Guardian/Interface may display these numbers. It does not raise them for free. Gains come from Cycling, Meridian Opening, Core Refinement, Body Tempering, and (for Aeric) Siphon gains.*

* **Strength (STR):** Muscle density, kinetic impact, heavy weapon threshold.
* **Dexterity (DEX):** Reflexes, fine motor control, weapon handling, stride precision.
* **Vitality (VIT):** Physical toughness, stamina pool, natural health regen, pain tolerance under load.
* **Perception (PER):** Threat reading, sensory acuity, Scan clarity, noticing tells and openings.
* **Intelligence (INT):** Pattern recognition, technique comprehension, Aether theory, Analytical processing speed.
* **Wisdom (WIS):** Judgment under pressure, Intent/Aspect insight, long-term path sense, resisting soft-cage choices.
* Baseline human average is **10**. No free attribute points.

## 3. Cultivation Attributes (Internal)

* **Meridians Opened / Cleared:** `X / 108` — primary progress tracker within the Unforged Realm and beyond.
* **Clogging:** Unforged baseline is 90%+ blocked by modern toxins and disuse.
* **Dantian Binding (%):** How stable/packed the host’s claim on their bound Aether is. Early Unforged = **Seeded / Unstable**. Copper Core = first true **stable Core** (bound Aether condensed enough to hold under real load).
* **Active Aspect:** Conceptual identity of the cultivator's bound Aether. Aspects advance through comprehension stages (e.g., Unfurl → deeper unfoldings).
* **Cycling Methods:** Personalized engines for moving Aether (e.g., Devouring Cycle). Methods may be Unrefined until stabilized through repeated use and Meridian clearance.
* **Aether Reserves / Currency:** Internal **bound** stores and spendable Aether for upgrades via the Guardian as Upgrade Auditor — **not** a Shop UI.
* **Impure Aether Sediment:** Toxic sludge deposited by low-tier / corrupt siphons (failed bind-break). Must be burned off by manual cycling or risk Meridian Rupture.

*Do **not** use Gaseous / Liquid phase language for Dantian state. Bound Aether is ethereal, not a gas canister.*

## 5. Progression Paths (No Classes / No Classers)

Power is built only through:

* **Cycling** — dragging raw Aether through the meridians into the Dantian
* **Meridian Opening** — clearing toxin clog through agonizing friction
* **Dantian Core Refinement** — advancing Realm / Core density
* **Intent / Aspect Comprehension** — shaping the conceptual identity of one's Aether
* **Siphoning (Aeric path)** — predatory extraction from kills / defeats (see §6)

There are **no System Classes**, **no Classers**, **no skill slots**, and **no free stat handouts**.

### How techniques & Aspects are earned

* **Theory + Execution:** New abilities are not handed out by System level-ups.
* **Mariusz** provides ancient academic formulas, meridian diagrams, and Aether-dynamics theory (Vellath Arch-Scholar knowledge).
* **Aeric** provides brutal physical execution, trial-by-fire testing, and high-pain endurance.
* Every new technique / trait / Aspect fragment: **effect breakdown + short Mariusz monologue**, then sync `@mc_sheet.md`.

### Foundation Siphon — core extraction types

Upon killing or defeating a target, Aeric’s Dantian may forcibly rip away foundation pieces:

* **Meridian Theft:** Siphon pure meridian alignment to force his own 108 pathways open faster than manual friction alone.
* **Trait & Aspect Siphoning:** Extract fragments of unique body refinements, elemental affinities, or physical traits. Incomplete fragments need Mariusz theory + further kills to stabilize.
* **Core Harvest:** Extract physical Aether cores/seeds from beasts for future crafting or cultivation fuel.
* Not free stats. Not a loot menu. Always costly in pain, sediment risk, and attention.

### Painful Reconstruction (Siphoned Healing)

* Siphoning does **not** heal like a health potion.
* It forcibly drags raw, unrefined vitality out of a corpse and **cauterizes / re-binds** torn muscle, skin, and bone.
* **Agony:** Feels like molten wire sewn through open nerves.
* **`[Stubborn Will]`** scales with damage load to keep him conscious while the body is forcibly re-stitched.
* Injuries still persist if he doesn’t pay the cost — no hand-waved recovery between scenes.

### Aether Sediment & Backlash (The Toxin Wall)

* Siphoning low-tier, corrupt, or unrefined beasts deposits **Impure Aether Sediment** into the Dantian.
* Back-to-back low-tier siphons clog meridians and threaten **Meridian Rupture**.
* He must spend time **manually cycling** to burn off sediment through sheer agony before safely siphoning higher-tier targets.
* Mariusz needles this constantly — efficiency vs greed, theory vs corpse-stuffed idiot.

### Philosophical edge (power use)

* Aeric does not slaughter innocents for fun.
* Soft rules of the old world are dead. Anyone who stands between him and survival / resources can become **raw material for his Dantian**.
* Ruthless pragmatism + Analytical Eye math; dark thrill under the fear.

## 8. Synergy (Frame + Fuel)

* Body attributes provide the **frame**. Cultivation provides the **fuel**.
* *Example:* High Strength + few opened Meridians = powerful hits, but slow energy delivery (telegraphed techniques).
* *Example:* Cycling through clogged Meridians is excruciating — Aether scrapes through like glass dragged through veins.
* *Example:* Foundation Siphon without sediment burns = short-term power, medium-term rupture risk.
