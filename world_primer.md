# World Primer: Day Zero — Book 1 Bible

> **AUTHOR-FACING ONLY.** Do not dump these facts into narration. Aeric and the reader discover the world through experience, confusion, and Mariusz — never through "old Earth" / Integration exposition.

## The Setting

* The world has been **newly integrated** into the System — and the soft age ended in a single instant.
* **Day Zero sequence (canonical order):**
  1. Technology dies. Vehicles crash. Raw **Aether** floods the air. The planet begins expanding to **1000x** prior size.
  2. **All humans are pulled into staging voids** for Guardian assignment and path selection *(tutorial)*.
  3. Hosts are **ejected back** into the expanded wilderness, **scattered** — prior proximity is not guaranteed.
  4. Monsters spawn. Clearing begins. Some freeze and die. Some fight. A few thrive.
* Modern cities and technology are erased. What remains is a primordial, untamed, **monster-ruled wilderness** choked with raw Aether.
* There are **no safe havens**, **no cities**, **no shops** on Day Zero Earth. Humans survive strictly on their own merits.
* **Why no Shop (cosmic reason):** Earth is a **Mortal Strata nursery / tutorial harvest world**. True System Trade Hubs and Shops operate in the **Ascendant Strata** and above. The cosmos does not build malls in kindergartens. Local Upgrade Auditor spend ≠ a Shop UI.

### Cosmic scale (author — do not dump early)

* **Tier 1 — Mortal Strata (Realms 1–6):** Foundation / Mortal Shell. Cap for tutorial worlds. Full realm table in `@system_mechanics.md`.
* **Tier 2 — Ascendant Strata:** True cosmos — Soul Forging, Laws, World-Cores, trade hubs, Shops.
* **Tier 3 — Architect Strata:** Conduit Lords / System framework entities. *(Not called “Sovereign Strata” — path name **Sovereign Plunder** stays distinct.)*
* To a fresh human, Gold Lord / Aether Sovereign feels like godhood. To high-tier cosmos, **6th Realm = finished primary school**.
* **Vellath grading (SPOILER tone):** Peak Vellath experts at 5th/6th thought themselves gods; the System logged the population as **Grade-1 Raw Conduit Material**. Fuels Mariusz’s bitterness. Imply; confess later.

## The State of Survivors

* Absolute isolation after ejection. Civilization is not collapsing — it is already gone.
* Everyone gets a Guardian. Nobody gets a free win.
* Soft humans freeze. Competent **Scaffolded Template** users scrape by. **Unsealed Manual** climbers treat the chaos as an open ladder.
* **Template nuance:** Standard Warrior/Weaver/Warden scaffolds buy early survival and typically **hard-cap inside Mortal Strata** (~3rd–4th Realm). Rare high-tier / broken scaffolds may push further — leave flex for Act III warlords without making Templates cosmically competitive long-term.
* **Unsealed (Aeric):** Agonizing Sovereign Plunder path keeps the foundation open toward Ascendant+ — not just to win the forest, but to leave the nursery.
* Human conflict escalates once survivors collide: template warlords vs unsealed predators vs idealists trying to rebuild soft law.

## Aether

* **Aether** is the universal energy in two forms:
  * **Ambient** — unowned world-fuel in the air (thick copper feeling). Cyclable by anyone who can draw.
  * **Bound** — ethereal personal fuel; **natively usable only by its host**. Held in the Dantian. Theft requires Null/Siphon to break the bind.
* **No Shop / Marketplace.** No gaseous/liquid phase language for Dantian state.
* Full siphon / sediment / reconstruction / bind rules live in `@system_mechanics.md`.

## The Magic Reality

* There are **no System Classes**, **no Classers**, **no skill loadouts**, and **no free stat handouts**. Power is built by hand.
* Power belongs to those who learn **manual cultivation**: Cycling raw Aether through **108 Meridians**, filling the **Dantian**, refining a Core, and forging an **Aspect**.
* Unforged mortals begin with 90%+ Meridian clogging. Clearing them is agony. Most will flinch and die.
* Guardians may offer **standard templates** (Warrior, Weaver, Warden, etc.) as handed-down stabilization scaffolds. Soft cages with hard caps **inside the Mortal Strata** (usually mid-tier). The unsealed/manual track is agony and keeps Ascension possible.
* Aeric Ryder walks the **Path of the Hollow Origin** — Absolute Void / Entropy — and Cycles by **Devouring**. Soft humans are livestock when they choose to be. The ladder is endless. He wants the top.
* Path evolution (author): `[Null Cycling]` → `[Foundation Siphon]` → Pilfering Aspect (**Rust & Void**) → long-term **Sovereign Plunder**. Details in `@system_mechanics.md`. Foreshadow; unlock on sheet when earned.
