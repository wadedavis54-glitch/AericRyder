# World Primer: Day Zero — The Expansion

> **AUTHOR-FACING ONLY.** Do not dump these facts into narration. Aeric and the reader discover the world through experience, confusion, and cold Guardian jargon — never through "old Earth" / Integration exposition.

## The Setting

* The world has been **newly integrated** into the System — and the soft age ended in a single instant.
* **Day Zero sequence (canonical order):**
  1. Technology dies. Vehicles crash. Raw **Aether** floods the air. The planet begins expanding to **1000x** old Earth.
  2. **All humans are pulled into staging voids** for Guardian assignment and path selection *(tutorial)*.
  3. Hosts are **ejected back** into the expanded wilderness, **scattered** — prior proximity is not guaranteed.
  4. Monsters spawn. Clearing begins. Some freeze and die. Some fight. A few thrive.
* Modern cities and technology are erased. What remains is a primordial, untamed, **monster-ruled wilderness** choked with raw Aether.
* There are **no safe havens**. Humans survive strictly on their own merits.

## The State of Survivors

* Absolute isolation after ejection. Civilization is not collapsing — it is already gone.
* Everyone gets a Guardian tutorial. Nobody gets a free win.
* Soft humans freeze. Competent scaffold-users scrape by. Unsealed climbers treat the chaos as an open ladder.

## Aether

* **Aether** is the universal energy.
* It is raw ambient fuel in the air, the currency of the Guardian's Shop/Marketplace, and the resource expended during cultivation, upgrades, and cycling.

## The Magic Reality

* There are **no System Classes**, **no Classers**, **no skill loadouts**, and **no free stat handouts**. Power is built by hand.
* Power belongs to those who learn **manual cultivation**: Cycling raw Aether through **108 Meridians**, filling the **Dantian**, refining a Core, and forging an **Aspect**.
* Unforged mortals begin with 90%+ Meridian clogging. Clearing them is agony. Most will flinch and die.
* Each host meets their assigned **Guardian / Conduit** in a personal staging void — impersonal Interface, Shop/Marketplace, and Upgrade Auditor. It measures and trades; it does not parent.
* Guardians may offer **standard templates** (Warrior, Weaver, Warden, etc.) as handed-down stabilization scaffolds. Soft cages with hard caps. The unsealed/manual track is agony, uncapped, and preferred by efficiency-maximizing Guardians when the host survives it.
* Aeric Ryder walks the **Path of the Hollow Origin** — Absolute Void / Entropy — and Cycles by **Devouring**. Soft humans are livestock. The ladder is endless. He wants the top.
* His `[Null Cycling]` is the seed of later **Pilfering Cultivation** — theft of Aether/progress beyond what his Realm should allow. Foreshadow only until unlocked.
