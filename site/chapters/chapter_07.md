# Chapter 7 — Overheard

The scream's echo had already gone cold by the time Aeric found the place it might have come from.

Not a body—worse, in a way. **Evidence.**

Trampled fern. Blood spray on a root-face at thigh height— arterial, recent enough to still smell metallic under the Aether copper. A scrap of blue fabric snagged on thorn. Template glow residue, faint and sickly, like someone had channelled wrong and the forest had noticed.

No corpse. Whatever had lost the fight had been **dragged**.

*Winner took the pantry.*

Aeric crouched. Analytical Eye traced drag marks—heavy, intermittent, claws not shoes. Not human footsteps leading away. The prey had been human. The collector wasn't.

"Don't follow," Mariusz said.

"I'm not stupid."

"You are curious. Curiosity and stupid share a bed in Unforged meat."

*Fair.*

He memorized the angle and backed out. Bone knife at his belt. Cord-wrapped spear. Leaf wraps riding his ribs—cooked eel almost gone, vermin strip still waiting for fire he didn't have on this slope.

*Find camp. Boil or char. Don't eat raw lottery.*

---

The fight found him before camp did.

Not his fight—**overheard**, like the title of a bad podcast he couldn't unsub from.

Voices first. Two. Three. Distance wrong again—close enough to parse tone, not words. Then the crack of something hard hitting something softer. A woman's shout—anger, not fear. A man's laugh that had **template** in it, the way Jonah's channeling had had template in it.

Aeric dropped into the fern line and crawled to a root-shelf that overlooked a shallow bowl in the forest floor.

Three humans.

Two standing. One on the ground.

The one on the ground didn't Scan clean—face down, blood pool spreading, template residue sputtering out like a bad battery.

Dessa kicked the body's shoulder. "Get **up**. You don't get to waste a Warden slot on a tantrum."

"He tried to run," Cole said. Not sorry. Not angry. **Bored.** "We said share the seep. He said no. Simple."

"He said no because you wanted **tribute**, not share."

"Same thing at Level one."

*Template kids playing warlord. Day Zero week one.*

Aeric's hand tightened on the spear. Not intervention hand. **Inventory** hand.

*Three templates. One dead or dying. Two armed. Six channels. Trace sediment. Half a meal. No glow. No allies.*

Dessa's head turned— not toward him. Toward the drag marks from the morning. Perception caught the micro-shift.

"Something's been eating near the north trough," she said. "Could be the same thing that took Henn's arm yesterday."

*Henn. Name on a corpse I haven't met.*

"We finish here," Cole said. "Strip what scans. Move before dark-thin."

Dark-thin. They had words for the wrong light too.

Dessa knelt. Palm on the downed man's back. Channel flicker—Warden scaffold trying to **Audit** something from a host that wasn't cooperating anymore.

*Not your business. Not your rescue.*

Aeric's weight stayed flat. Breath shallow. Wade's old mat voice: *You don't jump into someone else's roll unless you know the belt rank.*

The Audit failed. The body jerked once and went still.

"Dead," Dessa said. "Waste."

"Currency?" Cole asked.

"Three. Maybe four if we're lucky." She stood. "Strip the shoes. Leave the rest for beetles."

*Currency. They know the Auditor language. Templates come with training wheels.*

They worked fast—professionals in the way office thieves were professionals. Shoes off. Belt gone. A ring that caught template-light and went dark when Dessa pocketed it.

Aeric watched.

*Could've been Jonah. Could've been me if I'd said hello back.*

Cole kicked the corpse once—casual, sport—and spat into the fern. "North trough. We check the drag."

They moved out.

Aeric stayed until their footfalls died. Counted to sixty. Counted again.

Then he descended into the bowl.

The dead man was young—twenties, office haircut gone feral, fingers torn where he'd clawed dirt. No name on the Interface until Aeric forced Scan on a still-warm corpse.

<p><strong>Note:</strong> Weaver Template *(Scaffolded)* — channel collapsed; non-hostile *(dead)*</p>

*Weaver. Like Jonah. Like the kid who screamed while I ran pack math.*

He didn't touch the body for siphon. Humans were **fuel** in Mariusz's coldest lectures, but the hollow didn't reach for Renzo on its own—not yet, not without a choice he wasn't making in a blood bowl with two templates walking north.

*Not your first human leech. Don't make it easy to pretend it's different.*

"Leave," Mariusz said. Quiet. "You're not their enemy today. You're not his rescue either. **Move.**"

Aeric moved.

---

Camp was the bus grave again—he'd looped wrong or the forest had looped him, same torn panel, same ash pit, same metal ghost under root-mat.

*Commute tomb. Home.*

Fire took less time the second time—foam tinder dry-ish from shelter, kindling stacked, chrome on quartz. Sixth strike. Flame.

The vermin strip charred ugly. He ate it anyway. Better than raw. Not good. **Fed.**

*Second cooked beast. Still gross. Still counts.*

He refilled the stone cup at the bus seep, boiled, drank, packed reserve. Thirst managed. Hunger reduced—not gone.

*Templates are forming gangs. Renzo is dead. Dessa and Cole are north of the drag marks.*

Mariusz said, "Record it. Human conflict is **resource conflict** now. Scaffolded hosts learn Auditor language faster than unsealed meat. They will take seeps, kills, and corpses. You are one corpse if you moralize at the wrong knife."

*I'm not moralizing. I'm counting.*

"Count faster."

---

The Gnarl-Hound came at dusk-thin—same species as the wash fight, different individual, ribs showing, hungry enough to be brave.

*Level two. Gut seam. Bone knife for finish if spear binds.*

It lunged. Aeric slipped lateral—rusty footwork, BJJ angles without the mat—and speared the gut seam. Give. Ichor. Not deep enough.

The hound twisted. Jaw snapped where his forearm had been open since Day Zero. Pain—bright, old wound reopened.

*Not the arm. Finish.*

He dropped, hooked the near leg the way he'd hook a shot in the gym before Wade quit, and **rode** the twist into top position—heavy, ugly, **mount**.

*Don't give your back. Hip pressure. Breathe.*

Bone knife in his off hand. He drove it into the soft seam under the ribs where the spear had opened the line.

Dying. Not dead.

Separate beat.

Palm off the knife. Flat against the gut seam. Bare skin. Direct contact. Deliberate.

The rush hit—dark, warm, **good**, stronger than the vermin because the hound was **bigger**, a tide of yes that made his vision narrow to the contact point and the hollow drinking.

Three beats. Four. Five—

Something **dense** rose under his palm—the core-knot, pulsing, **pulling back**.

His fingers **dug**.

*Take it. Take—*

"**Stop.**" Mariusz's voice cracked like a whip. "You are **not** Foundation Siphon. You are seven channels and trace sediment and an arm that's weeping again. The core is **foundation**. You rip that now and you drink mud until something ruptures. **Residue. Only residue.**"

Aeric's jaw locked. The pleasure warred with the order—want vs math.

He ripped back on six.

Cold where heat had been. Wrong. Empty.

The hound went still.

*That almost—*

"Almost is how meridians ** explode**," Mariusz said. "File the hunger. Foundation Siphon is when you learn to take the seed without drowning in the loam. You are **not there yet**. Seven channels is still kindergarten."

Friction burn. Six paths. Outward spin. Molten-wire drag until his teeth ached and the hollow **coughed** sediment in three ugly vents that tasted like pennies and rot.

Not clean. Clean enough.

Painful Reconstruction came after—arm first, where the jaw had reopened the old Cur lines.

Molten wire. Agony after the good leech. Stubborn Will climbed. Tissue **knit** wrong-fast, weeping stopped, scab pulled tight over meat that still remembered being torn.

*Theft felt good. Fixing it feels like punishment. Both true.*

He didn't Reconstruct the shin from the vermin—that could wait. Fuel was finite. Math was math.

---

"Channel eight," Mariusz said when the fire was banked and Renzo's face had stopped floating behind his eyes.

"You said seven was kindergarten."

"Eight is kindergarten with homework."

Aeric sat. Spear across thighs. Bone knife sheathed at hip.

"Ambient draw. Thin. Seven paths."

Easy air.

"Eighth clog—spine base, low. Near the ass, near the root. You will hate it. Open anyway."

The Eye found it. Tight. Deep. **Mean**.

"Push. Hold—"

Pain.

Wire through meat. Birth-wrong. Capacity waiting on the other side.

He pushed on three. Held on four. The knot ** cracked**.

Eighth channel held.

"Two short of the cage admitting you might survive a template gang. **Different countdown.**"

*Dessa. Cole. North trough.*

---

Before full dark-thin, he climbed the bus roof panel—bad idea, good view—and saw **fire**.

Not his fire. Distant. Two dots, maybe three, miles wrong and close at once. Human pattern. Human camp.

Silhouettes moved against the glow—too far to Scan, close enough to know he wasn't alone in the math.

*Survivors forming. Gangs forming. Warlords loading.*

He didn't wave. Didn't shout hello.

*Last hello died alone.*

Aeric climbed down, checked the bone knife, and slept with the spear across his knees and Renzo's shoes-not-on-his-feet in his head.

*Eight channels. Templates strip corpses for Currency. Foundation Siphon is the next cliff.*

Mariusz watched the distant fires and said nothing that sounded like hope.
